The problem

The bus information exists. Turning it into a decision is the hard part.

Students moving between campus, PGs, sectors and the railway station keep running into the same three questions.

{{ icon('help', 24) }}

"Which bus even goes there?"

Timetables list routes, not answers. New students don't know the sector system yet.

{{ icon('stop', 24) }}

"Where do I get off?"

Missing your stop or taking the wrong direction is the most common mistake.

{{ icon('car', 24) }}

"I'll just take a cab"

When buses feel complicated, students pay 5–10× more for convenience.

What RouteMate does

Decisions, not timetables.
