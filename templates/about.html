RouteMate is an HCI course prototype. It turns the CTU bus timetable into simple choices for students.

Where it comes from

{{ icon('bus', 20) }}Routes & timings{{ source }}. {{ n_routes }} routes, {{ n_stops }} stops.
{{ icon('pin', 20) }}Stop locationsOpenStreetMap. Junctions like “Sector 22/17” sit between the two sector centres, so pins can be 100–300 m off.
{{ icon('map', 20) }}Maps & road shapes© OpenStreetMap contributors, roads via OSRM.

What is estimated

{{ icon('clock', 20) }}Bus times aren't liveDepartures are spread evenly between the first and last bus. Ride time assumes about 21 km/h.
{{ icon('rupee', 20) }}Fares are approximateCTU non-AC slabs: ₹10 up to 5 km, ₹20 for 5–10 km, ₹25 above 10 km.
{{ icon('car', 20) }}Cab prices are rough guessesDistance-based estimates, not prices from Uber, Ola or Rapido.

Privacy

{{ icon('shield', 18) }}Your location is used in the browser only to find nearby stops and plan from where you are. It isn't stored. Saved places, trips and feedback are kept on this prototype's server for the course evaluation.
