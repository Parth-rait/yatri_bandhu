Words we use

What is a “change”? {{ icon('chevron-down', 18) }}

Getting off one bus and taking another to finish your trip. Each bus needs its own ticket. “No changes” means one bus all the way.

What does “every 20 min” mean? {{ icon('chevron-down', 18) }}

A bus on that line comes roughly every 20 minutes. If you just missed one, the next is usually within that time.

What does “Best for you” mean? {{ icon('chevron-down', 18) }}

It's the route that best fits the priority you picked: cheapest, fastest, simplest or least walking. The others are still there to compare.

How do I know where to get off? {{ icon('chevron-down', 18) }}

Each bus step says “Get ready after …”, which is the stop just before yours. Start the trip and turn on live location for a reminder when you're close.

Are times live? {{ icon('chevron-down', 18) }}

No. They are estimated from the official CTU timetable (first bus, last bus, frequency). Buses can be a few minutes early or late.

How do I pay? {{ icon('chevron-down', 18) }}

Buy a ticket from the conductor on the bus. Fares shown are approximate CTU fares by distance: ₹10, ₹20 or ₹25.
